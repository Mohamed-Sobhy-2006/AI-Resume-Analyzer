# Deep Learning
Deep Learning uses multi-layer neural networks to learn from images, text, audio and other complex data.
Key topics to learn: neurons and activation functions, backpropagation, convolutional neural networks (CNN), recurrent networks, transformers, transfer learning, and regularization.
Recommended frameworks: PyTorch and TensorFlow with Keras. A GPU environment such as Google Colab or Kaggle notebooks is enough for learning. Deep Learning is required for computer vision, NLP and generative AI roles.
