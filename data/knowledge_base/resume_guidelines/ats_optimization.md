# ATS Optimization
Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) scan resumes for keywords before a human reads them.
- Use standard section headings such as Experience, Education, Skills and Projects.
- Mirror important keywords from the job description, for example Python, SQL or Docker.
- Use a simple single-column layout; avoid tables, text boxes, images and icons.
- Save the resume as PDF or DOCX and use a common font such as Arial or Calibri.
- Spell out abbreviations once, for example Natural Language Processing (NLP).
