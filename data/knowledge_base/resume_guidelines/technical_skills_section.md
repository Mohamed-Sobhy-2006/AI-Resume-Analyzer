# Technical Skills Section
The skills section is the first place recruiters and ATS look for keywords.
- Group skills by category: Programming Languages, Frameworks, Databases, Cloud and Tools.
- List only skills you can discuss in an interview and have used in a project or job.
- Put the skills required by the target job first.
- Avoid skill-level bars and percentages; they are subjective and are not ATS friendly.
- Add soft skills such as communication and teamwork inside experience bullets rather than a long list.
