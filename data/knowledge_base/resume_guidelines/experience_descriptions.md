# Experience Descriptions
Describe each role so that a reader understands your scope and impact.
- Format: job title, company, location, dates.
- Write 3-5 bullets per role; put the most relevant achievement first.
- Include internships, freelance work, volunteering and university team projects if you lack full-time experience.
- Match the language of the target job description without copying it.
- Keep tenses consistent: past tense for previous roles, present tense for the current role.
