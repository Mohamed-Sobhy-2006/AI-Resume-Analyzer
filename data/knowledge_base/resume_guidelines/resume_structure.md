# Resume Structure
A clear structure helps recruiters scan a resume in under 30 seconds.
- Header: name, email, phone, LinkedIn and GitHub links.
- Professional summary: two or three lines tailored to the target role.
- Skills: grouped by category such as languages, frameworks, databases and tools.
- Experience and projects: most recent first, with bullet points.
- Education and certifications: degree, university, graduation year.
- Keep the resume to one page for students and juniors, and two pages at most.
